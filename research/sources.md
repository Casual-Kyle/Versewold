# Research sources

Consulted 23 November 2026. Notes are original summaries and engineering proposals; no complete third-party essays, dictionaries, or modern poem texts were imported.

The [complete poetic-forms category survey](poetic-forms/catalog.md) adds all 149 direct entries of the supplied Wikipedia category. Its [inventory](poetic-forms/inventory.json) and [coverage manifest](poetic-forms/coverage.json) preserve article URLs, page identities, source revision attribution, classifications, and template mappings. The survey README states which full articles were reviewed and distinguishes Wikipedia-based coverage from independent specialist corroboration. Ignored local source extracts are research evidence; copied article bodies are not bundled into the application.
