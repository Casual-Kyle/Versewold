The bundled fonts are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their full copyright notices and licenses are included with the application assets:

- Cormorant Garamond — `public/licenses/cormorant-garamond.txt`
- EB Garamond — `public/licenses/eb-garamond.txt`
