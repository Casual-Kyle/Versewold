### Backup and edit-date rules

- **Three retained snapshots per poem and project manifest.** The retention constant has been reduced from 50 to 3. These are files on disk; the policy does not multiply the whole app or load a separate RAM copy for every revision. The three include the latest incoming text and up to two older versions. Existing excess snapshots rotate on that file's next changed save; this change does not perform a blanket cleanup of user data.
- **A poem's edit date means an actual change to its text.** Cursor/selection movement, search navigation, returning to a poem, opening another view, changing title/author/form/composition date, and editing project descriptions/tags/canvas positions must leave it unchanged. Search replacement, undo/redo that changes text, or restoring different text does advance it. An identical replacement is a no-op. New blank drafts acquire their first edit timestamp when their text first changes; existing recorded dates are preserved.
- **Composition dates remain separately editable.** The printed signature continues to use the composition date, not the last-edit timestamp.
- **Writing-position restoration is scoped to returning to a poem and is lower priority.** Store any future cursor/scroll information separately from document text and dates. Undo continuity may be added in the same later editor-state work; cross-session undo history is outside this iteration.
